# Electricity Pricing

The notes describe a simplified electricity market using a merit order and a marginal dispatched unit.

- Their stated order is renewables → nuclear → coal → gas. Treat it as the ordering in these notes rather than a universal ranking for every market.
- Market price equals the MC of the marginal unit in the stated model; fossil generation is described as typically setting that price.
- Quiz 3 writes “PS = Price − MC.” This is the note's abbreviated expression; it does not specify a total-output calculation.
- Under the Final sheet's “Gas Price Drop” example, off-peak prices fall while constrained peak prices stay unchanged in the short run.
- The long-run text in that section then refers to **lower build cost**, more plants and lower peak prices. That wording change is preserved rather than treated as a fully explained causal result.
- A separate build-cost-rise example says reduced entry raises peak prices more than off-peak prices.
