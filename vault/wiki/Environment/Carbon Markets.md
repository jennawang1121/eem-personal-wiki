# Carbon Markets

These notes connect carbon pricing, tradable permits and emissions moving between regions.

- Quiz 3 says a carbon tax raises fossil marginal cost, shifts supply and can induce fuel switching; the price effect depends on the marginal unit. It does not define a separate “fuel-switching price.”
- The notes describe offsets as additional compliance options that can lower permit and electricity prices.
- A free permit still has opportunity cost equal to its market price. The notes also discuss how allocation can affect exit decisions.
- For the two-firm exercise, **MAC_A = MAC_B = permit price**, with a total-abatement constraint. The notes describe more abatement by the lower-cost firm and calculate cost as area under MAC curves. The triangle formula belongs to those illustrated linear examples.
- **Leakage** changes where production occurs; **reshuffling** changes trading partners while production is unchanged.
