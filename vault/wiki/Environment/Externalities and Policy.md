# Externalities and Policy

The notes distinguish a private equilibrium from an outcome that includes external costs.

- Private equilibrium is written **MB = MPC**; the social optimum is **MB = MPC + MEC**.
- The notes associate negative externalities with overproduction. Their illustrated DWL calculation is **0.5 × (Q_market − Q_social) × MEC**; no general validity beyond the source's diagram is established here.
- The listed responses are a Pigouvian tax for unpriced damages, information labels for imperfect information, and minimum efficiency standards for principal-agent problems.
- Their instrument-choice rule favors a tax or cap-and-trade when emissions are measurable, and a technology mandate otherwise.
- The policy table contrasts fixed price/uncertain emissions under a carbon tax with uncertain price/fixed quantity under a cap. These are the notes' simplified categories.
