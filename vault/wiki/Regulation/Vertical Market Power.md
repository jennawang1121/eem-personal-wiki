# Vertical Market Power

The notes illustrate vertical market power through double marginalization.

- In their two-stage example, upstream and downstream firms each add a markup.
- Both stages have market power; the sheets describe them as two monopolists in a vertical chain.
- The result described is a higher final price and lower output, with deadweight loss mentioned in the Final sheet.
