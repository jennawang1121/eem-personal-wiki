# Storage and Arbitrage

The notes connect storage over time with commodity transport between locations.

- Carry costs include financing and storage costs. Quiz 1 says a higher interest rate reduces the incentive to store.
- Contango means futures prices exceed spot prices; backwardation means the reverse. The notes connect these to storage or selling incentives.
- The Final sheet gives the more specific storage test **F_T > S_0 × (1+r) + storage cost**. A futures price above spot alone does not include all the costs in this stated test.
- For transport, the notes state arbitrage occurs when **P₂ − P₁ > MC_transport**.
- The notes describe trade equalizing prices net of transport cost and transport market power sustaining an excessive price wedge.
