## Input

According to the notes, why can a natural monopoly lose money when the regulated price equals marginal cost?

## Displayed answer

When the regulated price equals marginal cost, the firm cannot cover its fixed costs [S3]. This results in a loss because the profit is less than zero, specifically equal to the negative of the fixed cost [S3].
