# Levelized Electricity Cost

Levelized Electricity Cost (LCOE) is defined as the constant price per MWh that results in a Net Present Value (NPV) of zero. The calculation depends on whether the marginal cost (MC) is fixed or variable.

*   If MC is fixed, LCOE is calculated as MC plus the Fixed Cost divided by the Discounted MWh.
*   If MC varies, LCOE is calculated as the sum of discounted costs divided by the sum of discounted total electricity.
*   A higher capacity factor leads to a lower LCOE because the fixed cost is spread over more MWh.
*   LCOE ignores time, location, and dispatchability, treating all electricity as equally valuable.
