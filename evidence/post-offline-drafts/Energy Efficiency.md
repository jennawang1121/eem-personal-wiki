# Energy Efficiency

Energy Efficiency involves several concepts related to behavioral failures and market mechanisms. The rebound effect describes a situation where increased efficiency leads to increased usage. Furthermore, the principal-agent problem can arise where the decision-maker is not the payer, such as a landlord not investing in maintenance while the tenant bears the utility cost.

*   Rebound effect causes efficiency increases to result in higher usage.
*   The principal-agent problem occurs when the decision-maker is not the payer (e.g., landlord not investing).
*   The Fuel Economy Standard (CAFÉ) requires fleet average MPG compliance through technology improvements or sales mix.
*   Credit trading is allowed in relation to the CAFE standard.
