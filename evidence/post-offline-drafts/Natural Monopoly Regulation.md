# Natural Monopoly Regulation

Natural monopoly occurs when economies of scale are large enough that one firm can supply the entire market at the lowest cost. This is identified when the Average Cost (AC) curve declines over the entire market demand range.

*   AC declining at market demand indicates a natural monopoly.
*   The relationship between Marginal Cost (MC) and Average Cost (AC) is defined as: MC < AC causes AC to fall, MC > AC causes AC to rise, and MC = AC corresponds to the AC minimum.
*   Regulatory options include setting price equal to marginal cost ($P=MC$), which is efficient but may result in the firm losing fixed costs (profit = $-F$).
*   A two-part tariff structure ($P=MC + \text{fixed fee}$) is mentioned, where the fixed fee can be used to calculate consumer surplus (CS).
