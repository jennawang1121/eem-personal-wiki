# Storage and Arbitrage

Storage Economics involves carry cost, which is the sum of the interest rate and storage cost. The relationship between futures and spot prices determines storage incentives: Contango (Futures price > Spot) provides an incentive to store, while Backwardation (Futures price < Spot) provides an incentive to sell now. Storage is intended to reduce price volatility across time.

*   Carry cost equals the interest rate plus storage cost.
*   Contango (Futures > Spot) creates an incentive to store.
*   Backwardation (Futures < Spot) creates an incentive to sell now.
*   Arbitrage occurs if the difference between prices ($P_2 - P_1$) exceeds the marginal cost of transportation ($MC_{transport}$).
*   Market power in transportation can lead to a deadweight loss due to a price wedge being too large.
