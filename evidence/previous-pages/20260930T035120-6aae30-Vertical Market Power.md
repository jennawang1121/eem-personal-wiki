# Vertical Market Power

Vertical Market Power occurs through Double Marginalization. This happens when multiple firms in a vertical chain each apply their own markup, leading to a higher final price and reduced total output. This situation only arises when both parties in the chain possess market power.

*   Double Marginalization involves two monopolists in a vertical chain.
*   Each monopolist adds a markup.
*   This results in a higher final price and lower total output.
*   It requires market power at both the upstream and downstream levels.
