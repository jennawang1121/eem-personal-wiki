# Futures and Hedging

The notes compare futures and forwards, then connect contract choice to hedging exposure.

- Futures are exchange traded and standardized. Quiz 1 mentions mark-to-market settlement, a clearinghouse that reduces counterparty risk, and high liquidity.
- Forwards are customized OTC contracts. The notes describe higher counterparty risk and lower liquidity than futures.
- The Final sheet defines basis risk as poor correlation between the futures contract and the actual asset.
- Future commodity purchases are hedged with long futures; future sales with short futures.
- The notes use **hedge size = exposure × price sensitivity**. Their solar-plant example gives 106,000 × 5 = **530,000 MMBTU short**, not a currency amount.
