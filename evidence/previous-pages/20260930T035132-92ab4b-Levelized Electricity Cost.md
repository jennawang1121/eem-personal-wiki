# Levelized Electricity Cost

The notes define LCOE as the constant price per MWh that makes NPV zero. The Final sheet specifies a constant real price.

- With fixed MC, the notes use **LCOE = MC + fixed cost / discounted MWh**.
- With variable MC, the formula divides discounted total costs by discounted electricity.
- Quiz 3 says “if capacity increases, LCOE decreases.” The Final sheet more specifically says **higher capacity factor** spreads fixed cost over more MWh. These phrases are not interchangeable; the sources differ in specificity.
- Both sources say LCOE ignores **time, location and dispatchability**, treating all electricity as equally valuable.
- The Final sheet distinguishes wholesale market price from the cost inputs used in the LCOE formula.
