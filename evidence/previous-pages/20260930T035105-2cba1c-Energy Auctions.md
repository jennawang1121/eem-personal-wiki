# Energy Auctions

The two sources compare uniform-price and pay-as-bid auctions, but their descriptions mix buyer and seller wording. Preserve that difference when revising.

- Quiz 1 says winners **receive** the clearing price in a uniform-price auction. The Final sheet says winners **pay** it. The source text alone does not resolve the auction-side convention.
- Both sheets describe competitive firms bidding approximately MC in the uniform-price setting.
- Pay-as-bid requires forecasting the clearing price; the notes describe bids near the expected price rather than MC.
- The stated response to a demand drop is part of the notes' auction example, not a separately verified general rule.
- The winner’s curse is discussed for common-value auctions, with more bidders increasing the risk of overestimation.
